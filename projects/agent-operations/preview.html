Metric definitions

Collection boundaries

Observations are read-only. Runtime identities use stable aliases. The collector does not read prompts, messages, process arguments, environments, passwords or access tokens.

Resources and health are sampled every 10 seconds. Missing accounting stays unavailable. Charts do not interpolate across missing intervals; aggregates use recorded checks rather than chart points.
